Deferred `Primer::Alpha::ActionMenu` menus reload their items when `src` changes on `<action-menu>` or `refresh()` is called. A primary menu rendered with `src:` now renders its loader in a `<template>` and an empty overlay body, so markup or specs that expect an `include-fragment` in the server-rendered menu no longer match, and a lazy menu carries `data-ready` before it first opens (it means no load is pending). A closed lazy menu applies a new `src` when it next opens, a preloaded or open one at once; a failed load retries on the next open. A response without menu items is discarded and fetched again on the next open. Form inputs keep their previous values while a load is pending or after it fails. A menu whose body other code emptied or replaced (for example a Turbo morph of the server's empty body) loads again, at once when open or preloaded. Fragments inserted into the menu body by other code are picked up, including those Idiomorph inserts before setting their attributes. Deferred menus sync the dynamic label and form inputs on every load, and a cloned or restored menu keeps its original label. Sub-menu focus zones and traps are released when the menu reloads or is removed.
